About

I combine hands-on software development with technical leadership across mobile, web, and backend systems. My work includes Kigo, an EV charging suite supporting approximately 15,000 drivers and growing; a charger management platform supporting approximately 300 chargers and growing across Singapore, Malaysia, and Thailand; and solar asset management platforms used by properties across Southeast Asia.

I led development of Kigo Bot, combining retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and custom Model Context Protocol (MCP) tools for customer support, charger diagnosis, and remediation. This reduced human intervention for issues starting or stopping charging sessions by an estimated 30%. Previously, I contributed to Novade’s web, mobile, and backend systems within a product portfolio serving more than 100,000 users across 20 countries.

I have led adoption of engineering best practices and AI-assisted development workflows, with experience in technical direction, mentoring, hiring, and configuring deployment and release pipelines.
